# After submitting: verify, process, refunds, notices, records

## E-verification (within 30 days of upload)

A return not verified within 30 days is treated as never filed; if verified late, the
verification date becomes the filing date (CBDT notification 5/2022). Do it immediately.

| Method | Needs | Good for NRIs? |
|---|---|---|
| Aadhaar OTP | Aadhaar linked to PAN and to a mobile that receives SMS | Yes if they kept the Indian number active (roaming SMS works) |
| Net banking | Indian bank's net banking -> e-file / income tax login | Yes, usually the easiest |
| EVC via pre-validated bank account | Bank account pre-validated and EVC-enabled on the portal | Yes |
| EVC via pre-validated demat account | Demat pre-validated | Yes |
| DSC | Registered digital signature | Rare for individuals |
| ITR-V by post | Print, sign in blue ink, send by ordinary/speed post to CPC, Bengaluru within 30 days | Fallback; slow from abroad |

After verifying, download: the **acknowledgement** (ITR-V marked e-verified), the full
**return PDF** (View Filed Returns -> download form), the **challan receipt(s)**. Store them
together with the computation sheet and statements.

## Processing u/s 143(1)

- CPC processes the return and emails an **intimation u/s 143(1)** (usually weeks, sometimes
  months). Check: total income, tax, interest, TDS credit, and the final line: nil demand,
  refund (with 244A interest), or demand.
- **Demand** -> compare line by line with the return. Common causes: TDS not matched (deductor
  filed late or wrong PAN), challan details mistyped, interest recomputed. Respond on the
  portal (Pending Actions -> Response to Outstanding Demand: agree / disagree with reasons)
  or file **rectification u/s 154** (Services -> Rectification) for a mistake apparent from
  the record. Don't ignore it: unpaid demands get adjusted against future refunds.
- **Refund** -> credited to the nominated pre-validated account. Track at Services -> Refund
  status or the NSDL/Protean refund tracker. A failed refund (account closed, name mismatch)
  needs a refund reissue request (Services -> Refund Reissue) after validating another account.
- **Defective return notice u/s 139(9)** -> respond within 15 days (extendable) under
  Pending Actions -> e-Proceedings, usually by filing a corrected return in response. Typical
  cause: wrong ITR form (ITR-1 by an NR), income not matching TDS head.
- **AIS mismatch / compliance e-campaign emails** -> respond with feedback or a revised/
  updated return if income was genuinely missed.

## Fix the root causes noticed during filing

- Mutual fund folios, demat and bank accounts tagged "resident" for an NRI -> update KYC to
  NRI, link the NRO bank mandate; then TDS will be deducted at source on future sales.
- Resident savings accounts -> redesignate as NRO (FEMA).
- TRC + Form 10F to banks to get treaty-rate TDS next year.
- Advance tax plan if large gains are expected (pay in the instalment after the sale).

## Post-filing record (template in worksheets.md)

Keep a one-page record per year, for next year's return and the other country's return:

- AY / tax year, form, section (139(1)/139(4)...), regime, residential status, day counts.
- Acknowledgement number and filing/verification dates (keep in private notes, not shared).
- Income by head and gain type, tax and cess, interest/fee, TDS by deductor, self-assessment
  challans (BSR, date, serial), refund/demand.
- Indian tax per item for the residence-country credit (tax + cess only).
- Errors hit on the portal and their fixes.
- Open items: folio tags, revisions, notices to watch for.
- Next year: trips to log, statements to download (and their passwords), deadline, new
  section numbers under the 2025 Act.
