# Disclaimer

This skill helps an AI assistant prepare and file an Indian income tax return with you. It
is not tax, legal or financial advice, and the assistant is not a chartered accountant.

- Tax law, rates, forms and portal screens change every year. Figures in this skill are
  labelled with the year they apply to and were checked in October 2026. India moved from the
  Income-tax Act, 1961 to the Income-tax Act, 2025 on 1 April 2026; section and form numbers
  differ between them. Always confirm current figures on incometax.gov.in or
  incometaxindia.gov.in before you rely on them.
- The e-filing portal's computation is what you pay. The scripts are estimators for checking
  your entries, with limitations listed at the top of each script.
- You are responsible for what you file and verify. Report income and days in India
  truthfully; keep the evidence.
- Treaty claims, foreign-asset reporting (Schedule FA / Black Money Act), residency cases near
  a threshold, property sales, business income and any notice from the department justify a
  review by a chartered accountant who handles NRI and cross-border returns.
- Your PAN, Aadhaar, date of birth and bank details are needed to open statements and fill the
  return. Share them only in a session you trust, and keep filing records private.
