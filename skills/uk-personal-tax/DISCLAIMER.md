# Disclaimer

This skill helps an AI assistant prepare UK personal tax returns, computations and correspondence. It is general information, not tax, legal or financial advice, and it creates no adviser-client relationship.

**You remain responsible for your return.** HMRC treats taxpayers as responsible for the accuracy of their returns, including where they used software or AI. An error from relying on this skill is, at best, a careless inaccuracy, which can carry a penalty of up to 30% of the tax understated (reducible to nil for unprompted disclosure).

**The rules are dated.** Figures were checked against GOV.UK and HMRC manuals on the date in `SKILL.md`. Rates, thresholds, form box numbers and HMRC practice change every year and sometimes mid-year. Check the cited source before relying on a figure, and check the current year's form notes before entering a box.

**New regimes carry more risk.** The FIG regime, the reformed Overseas Workday Relief and related claims began on 6 April 2025. Guidance and software support are still settling. Residence, split-year, share-scheme and cross-border cases are the most likely to need a qualified adviser (CIOT, ATT, ICAEW, ICAS or ACCA member). Free help: HMRC's online services and helplines, and LITRG guidance at litrg.org.uk.

**It does not file or connect to HMRC.** You, or your adviser, submit. Never share Government Gateway passwords with the assistant.
