# Pricing

## Evidence, strongest first

1. **eBay "Recommended price"** inside the listing form, based on recent sold items once category and condition are set. Capture it for every eBay item. Caveat: it averages the catalogue match, so a bundle or an unused premium variant can be dragged down by bare or cheaper variants (it suggested about a fifth of retail for an unused premium headset because the cheaper non-ANC model shares the name). Judge whether the comparison is like for like.
2. **Vinted's suggested price range** shown in the listing form. Often lower than desk estimates for branded boots and small electricals.
3. **eBay sold listings** (advanced search, "Sold items"). Automated fetching of eBay search pages is usually blocked; ask the person to check on their phone if it matters.
4. **Specialist used dealers** with published used prices (camera and drone dealers, for example MPB). Dealer resale is a ceiling for private sale; dealer buy price is a floor.
5. **Instant buyback quotes** (CeX, musicMagpie style). JS-heavy sites may not fetch. A quote sets the absolute floor.
6. **New price today** from retailers and price-comparison sites. The new price, especially a current discount, caps used value.

Asking prices on active listings are not sold prices; they run high.

## Heuristics that held up

- **Ceiling first.** Check for a cheaper re-release, a successor, or a permanent price cut. A re-released older model sold new cut the used value of the original by about 40%.
- **Condition is a price tier.** Unused and boxed is close to a discount-retail price, not a used price. Repricing unused items from "used" levels added the most money in the whole project.
- **Extras count.** Accessories found in photos (grips, tripods, spare lenses, hard cases) justify more than the camera-only figure.
- **Low use is a headline**: "flown fewer than 5 times", "worn twice". Put it in the title.
- **Disclosed faults lower the price, undisclosed faults cost more** (a dead internal battery: list as wired-only and drop the price about 10 to 15%).
- **Generational drops**: older Intel laptops and engraved accessories resell far below what the purchase price suggests.
- **Bulky, low-value, seasonal** items (fans in autumn, heaters in spring) sell locally and cheaply.
- **Hygiene items** (mattresses, used bedding, pillows) are liabilities online: donate. Animal rescues often take duvets charity shops refuse.

## Setting ask and floor

- Fixed price with offers, not auctions, for commodity items: auctions with one or two bidders close below market.
- Ask about 15% above the least they'd accept; set the auto-decline floor at that least amount.
- Vinted buyers almost always offer lower: list a little above the target.
- Colleague/friends prices can sit slightly under eBay (no fees, no postage, no returns risk), but only if the person wants that. Ask.

## The person's stance

Ask early: "fastest sale" or "full market price". People change their minds ("don't underprice anywhere", then "don't raise prices for colleagues", then "keep the updated prices"). Each time:
- Change only what they asked about.
- Show a before/after table with the total difference.
- Never change reserved or sold items' prices.
- Keep all channels (sheet, page, posts, listings) consistent, or say which differ and why.

## Planned drops

Write the schedule into the plan so nobody renegotiates daily, for example: full price for 2 weeks; drop 10% on a fixed date and repost once; accept best offers in the final week; anything left goes to a bundle, donation or the buyback floor.

## Category notes

| Category | Check before pricing or listing |
|---|---|
| Drones | Battery count, flights, swollen battery (never post a swollen lithium pack), region charger plug, local registration rules (see `channels-and-fees.md`) |
| Cameras / action cams | Lens scratches (photograph both lenses), all accessories, warranty card and receipt help |
| Phones, tablets, laptops | Sign out of iCloud/Google/Find My, factory reset, remove SIM; activation-locked devices get returned |
| Smart speakers, streaming sticks | Factory reset and remove from the owner's account, or the buyer can't set it up |
| Keyboards, wireless gear | Does wireless still work? Switch type |
| Skates | Manufacturer size vs shoe size (hockey skate sizes run about 1 to 1.5 below shoe size): give both |
| Boots and shoes | Size from the tongue/insole label; flex old midsoles for hydrolysis (crumbling = don't sell) |
| Office chairs | Gas lift holds height |
| Cordless tools | Battery still charges |
| Monitors, TVs | Collection only; couriered panels invite damage claims |
| Books / study texts | ISBN, edition year; only sell if the owner has finished with them |
| Imported items | Region model, warranty validity in the UK |
